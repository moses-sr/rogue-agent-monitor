What we learned

    Honest limits

    • Everything is simulated. We chose the honest error rate (15%); different settings change the numbers.
    • The Patient rogue still beats us: it stays clean for six rounds and is caught only 46% of the time. Our referee keeps its damage small even when it is not caught.
    • The referee costs one extra check on each disagreement, about 5 per game.
    • The rogues are scripts, not language models arguing their case.
